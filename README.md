### 🎙️ Speech Emotion Recognition in Moroccan Darija — Master's Final Project

Researched **automatic speech emotion recognition** for Moroccan Darija, comparing deep acoustic representations and machine-learning classifiers.

- Prepared an enriched corpus of **4,056 audio recordings** (16 kHz, mono) covering anger, happiness, neutral, and sadness.
- Compared **TRILL, VGGish, YAMNet, Wav2Vec 2.0, and WavLM**, together with MLP, SVM, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and KNN classifiers.
- Applied **Gaussian Data Selection (GDS)** in latent space to filter acoustic artifacts and stabilize training.
- **Selected pipeline:** TRILL + GDS + SVM.
- **Reported results:** **97.04% accuracy**, **97.03% macro-F1**, and **18 errors across 609 test samples**.

**Technologies:** Python · Speech Processing · Deep Learning · TRILL · scikit-learn · SVM
